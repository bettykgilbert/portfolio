Agent Assist AI Roadmap: Northstar Card Services

Prepared by Betty K Gilbert · October 2026

Sample only. Northstar Card Services is a fictional credit card issuer. All numbers are illustrative assumptions, shown at the bottom of the page.

Recommendation

Real-time Agent Assist can cut handle time and repeat calls on Northstar's two most costly call types, worth about $1.0M a year.

  • Start with convenience check fees. The guidance is mostly knowledge and simple math, so it can pilot in 6 weeks.
  • Follow with "why isn't my balance going down?" It needs statement data, but it returns more.
  • Prove each one first. Every use case pilots against a control group and scales only if it beats the baseline.

How each pilot is judged

  1. Matched groupsTest and control agents matched by site, tenure, and handle time, for 6 weeks.
  2. Targets set up frontHandle time and repeat calls must beat control. CSAT and accuracy can't drop.
  3. Adoption trackedLow use signals a training or prompt fix, not a failed idea.
  4. Weekly tuningReview sample calls and adjust prompts and wording.
  5. Go / no-go with leadersRecommend scale, extend, or stop to CX, Operations, Compliance, IT, and Finance.

Share every learning with GTM

Shorter calls are good. Fewer calls are better. What we learn from conversations goes to the go-to-market (GTM) teams for:

  • Upstream fixes, like clearer fee wording on the convenience check mailer and a "where your payment went" line on the statement
  • The IVR, so it answers these questions sooner
  • Digital assets, like app and web explainers, and later an AI agent that answers these questions directly

Proving value after launch

At 30, 60, and 90 days, report handle time, repeat calls, CSAT, and cost to serve against this business case, and recommend the next use case.

Illustrative assumptions

4.8M calls a year. Cost of $1.00 per agent minute. Avoided repeat calls are valued at that call type's handle time.

Use case 1: 192,000 × 1.5 min × $1 = $288,000 + 192,000 × 22% × 25% × 9.5 min × $1 = $100,320 ≈ $388K

Use case 2: 240,000 × 2.0 min × $1 = $480,000 + 240,000 × 18% × 30% × 10.5 min × $1 = $136,080 ≈ $616K

Balance example assumes about 23.8% APR (1.98% a month on $2,400). Autopay at $210 covers interest and typical purchases and lowers the balance.
